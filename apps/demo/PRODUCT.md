# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Someone who wants a video or its audio saved quickly, often on a phone or a
low-end device, on a slow or metered connection. They paste a link and expect a
file. They do not know what yt-dlp, Pyodide or a proxy is.

## Product purpose

Demo of yt-dlp-wasm: yt-dlp running in the browser with no server of its own.
The page also has to be a working tool. Success is a saved file with the right
name, and an error message that says what to do next.

## Direction

- Fast and light. Small JavaScript, system fonts, no framework.
- Plain words. Show each stage while it runs, and the log when something fails.
- One screen, one column. Settings and log stay collapsed until needed.
- Light and dark follow the system. Restrained color, one green accent.
- Keyboard use, contrast and reduced motion are requirements.
